Independent game studio in Aix-en-Provence, France ABOUT FLEXARION

Flexarion is a creative game studio based in Aix-en-Provence, born from the desire to craft French indie games backed by bold art direction and solid engineering.

01 / Identity & Genesis

Building worlds on our own terms

Behind Flexarion are three creators united by their passion for video games: Timéo Morsilli, Hugo Banti, and Enrique Ruiz.

The studio formed around a simple yet demanding ambition: creating our own space for game research and development, free from rigid templates, to explore original mechanics, rich atmospheres, and memorable stories.

As a young independent studio, we are laying the technical and artistic foundations of our identity. Every prototype, internal tool, and world concept contributes to that long-term craft.

02 / Philosophy

Where art meets systems

At Flexarion, game design, art direction, and engineering are never treated as separate silos. A gameplay mechanic tells a story just as powerfully as a cutscene; lighting and physics shape how a game feels in your hands.

We favor focused, legible, deeply atmospheric works over inflated promises. Taking time to prototype, listen to controller feel, and polish every interaction is at the core of our indie development philosophy.

04 / Creation method

From spark to playable world

Short iterative cycles centered on immediate feel and overall coherence.

STEP 01

Exploration & Greyboxing

Before heavy production, we isolate core gameplay loops in fast playable prototypes to validate feel, pacing, and fun.

STEP 02

Art Direction & World

We define visual grammar, architectural references, lighting, and sound identity so form and mechanics evolve together.

STEP 03

Development & Iteration

Building game systems, level design, regular internal playtests, and continuous balancing.

STEP 04

Polish & Optimization

Refining micro-interactions, frame-rate stability, UI clarity, and launch readiness across target platforms.
