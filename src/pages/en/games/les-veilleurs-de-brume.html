FLX-01 · Flagship production · Godot 4.3 THE MIST WATCHERS

In development · Target Q4 2027

Listen to what the mist hides

Perched atop limestone arches above a perpetual sea of clouds, the city of Ombrefalaise only reveals itself at dusk. Every night, ancient optical lighthouses ignite across its sunken districts, carving fleeting corridors through shifting fog that erases yesterday’s charts.

You play as Nara, a night cartographer equipped with an acoustic astrolabe and a prism lantern. By triangulating lighthouse beams and decoding resonant echoes left by vanished citizens, you choose which fragments of history survive in the Grand Atlas before the mist swallows them forever.

01 / Gameplay pillars

Three pillars at the core

Pillar 01 · Dynamic cartography

Chart the city live

Your map is never static: measure angles between beacons, sketch wind currents, and plot safe routes directly inside an interactive field journal.

Pillar 02 · Light & Wind

Carve through the fog

Rotate belvedere mirrors to slice through wind-reactive volumetric mist, uncover forgotten walkways, and power ancient mechanical relays.

Pillar 03 · Selective memory

Choose what endures

Each expedition demands narrative choices: your lantern can only preserve a limited number of acoustic echoes, shaping the upper city’s fate.
