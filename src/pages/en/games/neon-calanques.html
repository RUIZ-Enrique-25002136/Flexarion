FLX-02 · R&D Prototype · Unreal Engine 5 NEON CALANQUES

Exploration · Target 2028

The night belongs to the bold

In 2094, Marseille’s white limestone cliffs house tidal generators and suspended viaducts towering above a bioluminescent Mediterranean. When an unknown offshore signal destabilizes the harbor grid, only solar couriers can traverse the reefs before dawn.

Each run throws you into a kinetic vertical climb where momentum is your best shield. Chain cable grinds, wall-bounces, and light-creature captures mid-fight to mutate your loadout and trace the wave back to its source.

01 / Gameplay pillars

Momentum, light, and risk

Pillar 01 · Solar parkour

Preserve momentum

Magnetic grapple, limestone wall-runs, and suspended cable surfing: the faster you flow, the faster your abilities recharge.

Pillar 02 · Light symbiosis

Tame neon wisps

Capture luminous entities mid-run to fuse elemental modifiers onto your melee strikes and ranged bursts.

Pillar 03 · Day-to-night shift

A shifting coastline

Arenas transition from blinding Mediterranean noon to electric midnight shadows, altering enemy patterns and traversal shortcuts.
