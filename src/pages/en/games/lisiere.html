FLX-03 · R&D Prototype · Godot 4 LISIÈRE

Exploration · Target 2028

Memory grows toward the light

At the edge of a quiet pine forest and a village abandoned forty years ago, the trees respond to acoustic frequencies like living tuning forks. Every clearing holds a frozen memory waiting to be harmonized.

With no dialogue boxes and no timers, Lisière invites you to reposition acoustic stones, part the canopy to let sunbeams fall, and compose an organic polyphony that slowly unveils the shape of a vanished place.

01 / Gameplay pillars

Listen to solve

Pillar 01 · Spatial resonance

Sound as mechanics

Each standing stone modulates the pitch, filter cutoff, and reverb of a live synth pad to guide you toward harmonic alignment.

Pillar 02 · Living botany

Reactive clearings

Foliage blooms or retracts based on sunlight angles and acoustic harmony, uncovering hidden trails across the woods.

Pillar 03 · Wordless narrative

Zero mandatory text

The story unfolds strictly through light, ruin topography, and the forest’s musical evolution—universally readable in any language.
