LOG #03 FLX-03 · Lisière November 10, 2026 By Enrique Ruiz

Procedural Audio Architecture and Minimalist Synth Pads

In Lisière, music is never a static pre-recorded WAV loop: every acoustic stone placed in a forest clearing acts as a live DSP node driving an additive synthesis graph (detuned sine oscillators, resonant low-pass filter, and modulated delay line) powered by Godot 4 and PipeWire.

As the player approaches a puzzle’s harmonic solution, phase beating settles cleanly into a pure fifth before parting the canopy. The whole synth graph uses under 1.8% of a single CPU core and preserves seamless musical continuity between clearings.

LOG #02 FLX-02 · Neon Calanques October 14, 2026 By Hugo Banti

Art Direction: Translating Mediterranean Calanques Light into Cel-Shading

For the Neon Calanques prototype in Unreal Engine 5, we wanted to avoid generic grey sci-fi tropes. We started from noon photo studies of Marseille’s Sormiou cliffs: blinding white limestone sliced by crisp cobalt-blue cast shadows.

Our custom post-process pass quantizes diffuse luminance into three sharp steps while keeping HDR bloom intact on neon signage and light wisps. Ink outlines combine depth and normal-edge discontinuities, dynamically thinning during high-speed parkour so silhouettes stay readable at full momentum.

LOG #01 FLX-01 · The Mist Watchers September 18, 2026 By Timéo Morsilli

Generating Wind-Reactive Volumetric Fog in Godot 4

In The Mist Watchers, fog is not mere set dressing—it is the core navigation obstacle. We pair Godot 4.3’s volumetric FogMaterial with a 3D Worley-Perlin noise texture advected along a 15 Hz CPU wind vector field.

When Nara aims a lighthouse mirror or her prism lantern, a negative-density occlusion volume carves straight through the mist while injecting a warm scattering gradient. Capping froxel raymarching at 48 steps with temporal reprojection holds a steady 60 fps on a GTX 1060.
