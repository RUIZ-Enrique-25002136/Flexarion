01 / Flagship production

The Mist Watchers

A three-person team focused on one immediate goal: delivering a tightly crafted, atmospheric narrative adventure.

Suspended above a sea of drifting clouds, you play as Nara, a night cartographer tracking signals from ancient lighthouses. Every beam uncovers forgotten memories and reshapes the sunken city’s geography.

Engine
Godot 4.3 (custom volumetric shaders)
Genre
Narrative adventure & exploration
Platform
PC (Windows / Linux)
Target window
Q4 2027

02 / The studio

An independent studio driven by creative craft

Flexarion is an independent game studio in Aix-en-Provence, southern France. We craft human-scale works where art direction, sound, and engineering evolve as one.

Meet the team, our tools, and our method

01 — FOCUS

One primary production

As a trio, discipline means focus: our full production bandwidth goes into The Mist Watchers.

02 — CRAFT

Open & custom tooling

From Godot 4.3 to Blender and PipeWire pipelines, we build lean technologies tailored to each world.

03 — AMBITION

Local roots, global reach

Rooted in Aix-en-Provence’s creative scene, designing games from day one in both French and English.

03 / Manifesto

MORE THAN GAMES. EXPERIENCES.

We see video games as a total art form: an ongoing dialogue between code, light, sound, and interaction. Every world we build in Aix-en-Provence aims to spark lasting emotion and the pure joy of discovery.
