Frequently asked questions — Flexarion Studio FREQUENTLY ASKED

Everything you need to know about Flexarion, our roots in Aix-en-Provence, our upcoming games, and how to reach us.

Frequently asked questions

01Who is Flexarion?

Flexarion is an independent game creation and development studio led by Timéo Morsilli, Hugo Banti, and Enrique Ruiz. Our goal is to craft original interactive experiences combining solid engineering, distinct art direction, and creative freedom.

Learn more on The Studio or meet the founders on Credits.

02Where is the studio located?

Flexarion is based in Aix-en-Provence, in the Provence-Alpes-Côte d’Azur region of southern France, building games for both French and international players.

03What kinds of games are you making?

We develop independent games where world identity, tactile feel, and staging take center stage. Exact genres and full reveals will be announced on our Games page.

04Which platforms are you targeting?

Target platforms will be confirmed project by project based on each game’s technical scope and control ergonomics.

05How can I follow Flexarion’s progress?

Official updates and engineering notes are published on Games, in our Devlog (also via RSS feed), and through our demo waitlist. You can also follow us on Bluesky, YouTube, Itch.io, and GitHub via the footer or Contact page.

06Can we collaborate with Flexarion?

Yes! We welcome inquiries around artistic collaborations, music composition, publishing, press coverage, or indie studio exchanges via our Contact page.

07How can I reach the team?

Use our Contact form or email contact@flexarion.timeoms.fr.

Still have questions?

Write to us directly and we will reply as soon as possible.
