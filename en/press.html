Short bio

Flexarion is an independent video game studio based in Aix-en-Provence, France, founded by Timéo Morsilli, Hugo Banti, and Enrique Ruiz.

Full bio

Based in Aix-en-Provence in southern France, Flexarion is an independent studio dedicated to crafting distinctive interactive worlds. Combining software engineering, 3D modeling, and art direction, the team is currently developing three original titles: Les Veilleurs de Brume, Neon Calanques, and Lisière.
